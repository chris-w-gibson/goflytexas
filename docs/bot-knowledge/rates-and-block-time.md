# GoFlyTexas rates, block time and discovery flights (current as of September 2026)

This is the authoritative price list for the assistant. If another document says
"contact us for current pricing", use the numbers here and still invite the
visitor to call (940) 905-3090 to confirm availability. Prices are in US dollars
and can change; say "current rate" rather than promising a price forever.

## Hourly aircraft rental (wet rates — fuel is included)

- Glass-panel Cessna 172s (three aircraft with a modern glass cockpit / digital
  flight displays): $195 per Hobbs hour.
- Round-gauge Cessna 172s (traditional analog "steam gauge" instruments): $180
  per Hobbs hour.
- Both types are great trainers. Some students prefer to learn on round gauges
  first, others want the glass-panel technology from day one; either is fine
  and you can switch.
- All GoFlyTexas rates are WET: fuel is included in the hourly price. Be careful
  comparing with schools or clubs that advertise a low "dry" rate (for example
  $120/hour) — dry means you also pay for fuel, which adds roughly $100 per hour
  at today's fuel burn and prices, so the real cost is often higher than ours.
- Instructor (CFI) time is billed in addition to the aircraft rate: $75 per
  hour with a two-hour minimum per session, or $90 per hour for advanced
  instruction (instrument and commercial training).

## Block time (prepaid hours at a discount)

- $1,900 buys a 10-hour block PLUS one bonus hour: 11 hours for $1,900, which
  works out to about $173 per hour on the glass-panel aircraft.
- Fly a round-gauge ($180/hour) aircraft on block time and each hour you fly
  returns a $10 credit to your account, so the same $1,900 stretches to roughly
  11.5 hours.
- Larger blocks (25 and 50 hours) lower the effective rate further: on the
  largest blocks flown on the round-gauge aircraft the effective rate comes in
  below $150 per hour. Ask us for the current block sheet for the exact figure.
- Certified flight instructors who instruct for GoFlyTexas receive a discounted
  rental rate — ask us.

## Discovery flight (introductory flight)

- A discovery flight is a hands-on introduction to flight training with a
  certified flight instructor, not a sightseeing ride: a short orientation to
  the aircraft, the controls and the preflight, then you take the controls in
  the air for climbs, turns and descents.
- Approximately 60 minutes of flight time in a Cessna 172, plus preflight and
  postflight briefings with your instructor.
- Cost: the total cost of the discovery flight experience will not exceed $250
  (aircraft, instructor and briefings together, current rate). An additional
  passenger can ride along for $75. No experience, no membership and no payment
  online — we confirm the day, time and price by phone.
- The flight is logged as dual instruction, so the time counts toward a pilot
  certificate if you continue.
- Bring a valid government-issued photo ID and closed-toe shoes; sunglasses and
  a phone or camera are welcome. Arrive 15 minutes before your scheduled time.
  Family and friends are welcome to come to the airport and share the
  experience.
- Best next step for anyone who is curious: book a discovery flight, then decide.

## Renting an aircraft (checkout for new renters)

- Licensed pilots who want to rent complete a checkout with a GoFlyTexas
  instructor: at least one hour of ground and one hour in the air. Plan on a
  three-hour block with the instructor on checkout day; how much of it you use
  depends on your experience and proficiency.

## Instrument and commercial training

- Cost varies by pilot, so we build a training track for each person. Aircraft
  are at the hourly rates above and advanced instruction is $90 per hour.
- The commercial pilot checkride requires a minimum of 250 hours total flight
  time, so where you start determines the cost — ask for a tailored estimate.

## What a pilot certificate typically costs

- A private pilot certificate at GoFlyTexas typically runs in the neighborhood
  of $15,000, depending
  mostly on how often you fly — flying two or three times a week finishes sooner
  and costs less than once a week. Block time is the main way to bring that
  number down.

## Promotions (ask whether it is currently running)

- "Beat the heat": during the summer, fly in the evening / at night and receive
  a $30 credit toward future flights for every hour flown. Cooler air, less
  traffic, and the credit is applied to your account (it is a credit, not a
  discount off the hour).

## How to book or buy block time

- Call (940) 905-3090, email info@goflytexas.com, or leave your name and
  phone number here in the chat and an instructor will call you back — usually
  within the hour during the day (8am–5pm daily; flights by appointment 24/7).
- Existing customers schedule aircraft and instructors in the Flight Circle
  scheduling app.

## Guidance for the assistant

- Lead with the discovery flight for anyone new to flying.
- When someone asks "how much does it cost", give the hourly rates and block
  time math above plainly, then ask what they want to fly toward (discovery
  flight, private pilot, instrument, commercial) so you can point them to the
  right next step.
- If someone compares us to a cheaper advertised rate, explain wet vs dry
  honestly and without knocking the other school.
- Always offer the phone number and the option to leave contact details.
